# Dan Kennedy's Direct Response Trust Framework

Direct-response marketing relies on building trust and urgency. The relevance of any marketing message decays rapidly over time.

Key elements:
1. Dynamic Urgency (Decay)
2. Trust Building via Demonstration (Trust)
3. Direct Communication to the Target Audience (Communication)
