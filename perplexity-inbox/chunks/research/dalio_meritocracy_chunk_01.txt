# Ray Dalio's Idea Meritocracy Framework

An idea meritocracy is a system that brings together smart, independent thinkers and has them productively disagree to come up with the best collective thinking.

The core mechanisms are:
1. Radical Truth (Trust)
2. Radical Transparency (Trust)
3. Believability-weighted Decision Making (Systems)
