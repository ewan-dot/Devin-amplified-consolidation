Tier Definitions (Important)
Tier 1 (Major Process Groups / Level 1)
High-level categories that cover the entire business. These are broad and cross-functional. Usually 6–10 in total.
What makes it Tier 1: It affects multiple departments and is strategic in nature.
Tier 2 (Core Processes / Level 2)
Specific end-to-end processes within a Tier 1 group. These are what people usually think of as “a process”.
What makes it Tier 2: It has a clear start → end, produces a measurable output, and can be owned by one team or person.
Tier 3 (Sub-processes / Level 3)
Breakdown of a Tier 2 process into logical chunks.
What makes it Tier 3: Still meaningful but smaller — usually 3–8 per Tier 2 process.
Tier 4+ (Atomic Bricks / Level 4+)
The smallest repeatable, measurable, automatable units. These are your AI-native building blocks.
What makes it Atomic: It can be done consistently with high confidence (your 95% target), has clear inputs/outputs, and can be fully modelled in your graph + Vellum.
