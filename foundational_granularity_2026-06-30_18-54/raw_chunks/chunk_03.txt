### Why this structure respects Taguchi's principles
1.  **Strict Error Isolation at the Gate (Layer 2):** Any deviation at the base (e.g. file content altered without updating the YAML hash, database schema target misconfigured) is detected and blocked immediately by our Python gates before it can pollute the index (Layer 3) or feed corrupted contexts into the prompt sandwich (Layer 4).
2.  **Lightweight Mapping (Layer 3):** Databases do not store fuzzy text; they store pointers and hashes, keeping the ontological relationships clean, fast, and easy to audit.
3.  **Maximum Attention Scoping (Layer 4 & 5):** The compiled Logic Sandwich forces the LLM to process constraints first (highest attention zone) and methodologies last (recency zone), ensuring that its reasoning at the pinnacle is mathematically bounded by the rules at the base.
