The flashy, mustachioed unicycle-riding jugglers (hyped tech news, generic business advice, and superficial blog posts) are the noise. Here is how our architecture is engineered to act as the truffle dog:

### 1. Training the "Scent" (The Reasoning Primitives)
We don't try to make the AI guess what a truffle smells like from scratch. Instead, we program the "scent profiles" directly into the **Reasoning Primitives registry** using established, robust anchors:
*   **Nobel-grade & proven formulas:** We feed it the exact mathematics of Altman Z-Scores, Markov state matrices, and Taguchi loss functions.

### 2. Tagging the Forest (The Flat YAML Metadata)
The **17–20 YAML metadata fields** are the coordinates of the forest. They label the data neutrally with its source, domain, and mathematical crossover points. 
*   Because the labeling is neutral, we don't force a single interpretation on write.
*   If we label a chunk with raw, simple indicators (like quantity counts, time delta, and domain indicators), we keep it flexible.

*   By combining the simple facts that are "impossible to track by hand," the laser stabs the exact chunks and pins them to a specific decision spike.
